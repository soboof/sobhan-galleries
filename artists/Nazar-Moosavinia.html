StatementAbout the work

Poisonous forests

The carnivorous penguins swallowed their bitter saliva hardly.
They were listening to the voice of the sheets under their feet.
And they knew that Vahad is coming this time from Baghdad.
As though all the west winds
Have kissed the redness of your larynx, remember?
When you were died,
The poisonous forests multiplied the air more and more.
I had a dream last night,
My father sold my body in one of Samorra's bazaar.
Then I reproduced somewhat that I couldn't feel the difference of any body You were there
In front of the altar
The balance of your body was not related
To the weight of the pistol that was shaking in your frozen hand
The dusty tortoises were warming their big lips with heat of your body
If it was the second night
I've dreamed again
A leprous man in a ruin, was drawing the map of his country on nakedness of my corpse. My mother was saying always
My corpse smells like fetid flowers
And I've forgot my killer's name.
I can't remember anymore
I've sold my body for how many rotten coins this time.

Nazar Moosavinia

The characters represented in these paintings are a collection of animal like humans and human like animals; they went through hardships of all kinds, time after time. Wounded, sometimes even dead, they were abandoned to their fate: standing naked and truthful in front of the viewers .
