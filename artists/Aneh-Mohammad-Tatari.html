StatementAbout the work

Traditional art, is to idolize God. It can't be called traditional if not for the presence of God.

In Iranian abstract paintings, it's expressed as light as well as in Yin Yang (Chines beliefs) which appears as ray in darkness.

As the poet Sohrab Sepehri says:"Eyes should be cleansed."So let us take another look at this traditional art which bears a universal connection.
