StatementAbout the work

Relationship

Relationship! Nowadays this is a word which we face and confront cautiously. A matter which can be candidly said that has been one of the oldest and most crucial human achievements and is so important and intermingled with human life that he spends a great proportion of this life making various tools and ways of building a relationship. But presently sometimes it seems that in crucial points in human life we are afflicted with misunderstandings in the components of relationships; senders and receivers tend to repel each other, massages mix and are misinterpreted. Feedbacks are lost in nowhere instead of moving toward their true destination, or they are intentionally shuttled and the atmosphere or grounds for relationships are sometimes contrary to or alien to the very matter of relationship. Sharing in today's human relationships has given way to the extremely strong caution signs of specifying private territory. And all these put together have made the 20th century and the present century very much noticeable and outstanding. Hani Najm is one of the young painters who has very well understood and grasped this meaning. With his specific style and approach towards the subject has tended to reflect these inadequacies and common pains in his paintings. In all these paintings which are related like a homogeneous meaning system, Hani Najm with exercising expressionism style, deformed figures and using specific dull colors and monotone spaces of busses or public vehicles tries to demonstrate a state which definitely is not good and is even deformed and abnormal and is a transition of a humane-relationship pain and feeling. The atmosphere of all paintings is a crowded and alien environment of public transport means which is a symbol of transportation and transition itself. And humans within these environments as representatives of members of a society in this space are afflicted with some kind of unfavorable changes and alienation in their face gestures, even their complexions and glances and the form of their body parts. Anxiety and worry can so obviously be seen in some faces and some kind of indifference in some others that is maybe the natural outcome of ((escape from freedom)) and responsibility!

There is no doubt that Hani Najm in this group of canvases and exhibitions that are connected like circles have had a look at some of the fundamental human and existential concepts. Even these connected circles of his works have been manifested and reflected in some of these works. Heads with deformed and upset eyes and mouths that are rolling like consecutive and linked rings, or in another canvas; circles in front of the foot of people; the more they move from a green atmosphere- which symbolizes nature- towards a red one- which would be a representative of violence and becoming municipal - in a horizontal (bus-like) way, the fewer these circles become and eventually they disappear, this could mean the incompetency of human relationships and behavior; and the incongruity of being in various forms of beings in today's human beings. This unity between an individual and environment can appear as a feeling for an individual. That means that when an individual feels he\she is estranged from his\her self and this world, he\she no longer experiences this feeling and instead, he\she is afflicted with anxiety and hopelessness.

Amir Aghajani
